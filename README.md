# Aeitron AI Automation Agency OS

> An AI-powered Agency Operating System built to manage finance, clients, invoices, leads, teams, automation, and business intelligence from a single unified platform.

## Overview

Aeitron AI Agency OS is a comprehensive business management platform designed for modern agencies and service-based companies.

The platform centralizes agency operations by combining financial management, client relationship management, invoicing, lead generation, team collaboration, AI-powered assistance, and business intelligence into a single dashboard.

Instead of using multiple disconnected tools, agencies can manage their entire workflow through one integrated system.
